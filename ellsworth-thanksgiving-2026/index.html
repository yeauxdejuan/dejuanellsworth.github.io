A few helpful answers

Before you head over.

A few essentials for the gathering. The family group chat is the place for confirmed travel details and questions.

Where are we staying?

Friscoville Mansion in Arabi, Louisiana, in the New Orleans area. See the house section for photos. Ask in the family group chat for the address and directions.

What about check-in, check-out, and parking?

Ask in the family group chat for confirmed check-in and check-out times, parking instructions, and arrival details. Add your own arrival and departure details to your RSVP. All times on this site are Central.

What should I bring?

Visit the menu to see what’s covered and sign up for a dish or supplies. Ask in the family group chat about anything else to pack.

How do I share food allergies or dietary needs?

Add them in the optional field on your household’s RSVP. Joined family members can see these details to help plan the meal.

Can I change my RSVP later?

Yes. Return in the same browser and choose Edit my RSVP. You can update your guest count, travel details, or dietary needs. If you’re using another device, ask in the family group chat before creating another household RSVP.

Who should I contact with a question?

Post in the family group chat for help with the invitation code, travel arrangements, or anything else about the gathering.
